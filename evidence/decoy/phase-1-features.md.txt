# OliveBloom — Phase 1: MVP Feature List

## 1. Product Name

**OliveBloom** is the working product name for the Argonz mobile app: an AI chat application for thoughtful conversations, available on iOS and Android, with first-class light and dark modes.

## 2. Vision

OliveBloom gives people a calm, focused place to think out loud with an AI companion. Rather than optimizing for volume of messages or novelty, OliveBloom optimizes for the quality of a conversation: clear history, comfortable reading in any lighting, and a chat experience that feels intentional from the first screen.

In Phase 1, OliveBloom ships a complete, dependable chat loop — sign in, start a conversation, keep your history, and personalize the look of the app — plus the administrative basics needed to operate it.

## 3. Goals (Phase 1)

- G1: A new user can create an account and send a first message in under two minutes.
- G2: Conversations persist reliably across sessions and devices for a signed-in user.
- G3: Light and dark modes are fully supported, with a system-follow default.
- G4: Operators can manage users and monitor core usage without engineering involvement.
- G5: The billing foundation (credits and consumable in-app purchases) is defined and ready for Phase 2 rollout, per the billing documentation.

## 4. Non-Goals (Phase 1)

- Multi-agent or tool-using AI behaviors beyond a single conversational model.
- Social features (sharing conversations, public profiles, community feeds).
- Web or desktop clients.
- Subscription plans, team accounts, or enterprise administration.
- Rich media generation (images, audio) inside chat.

## 5. Product Principles

1. **Conversation first.** Every screen either starts, continues, or organizes a conversation.
2. **Calm by default.** Motion, color, and typography stay restrained; the AI's voice is helpful, not performative.
3. **Respect the reader's light.** Light and dark modes are equal citizens, not an afterthought.
4. **Nothing is lost.** History is durable, and destructive actions require confirmation.
5. **Small surface, solid edges.** We prefer fewer features that work everywhere over many that work sometimes.

## 7. Billing Note

Monetization in OliveBloom is built on a credit system purchased through **RevenueCat-managed consumable in-app purchases** on iOS and Android. Credit packs, balances, spend rules, receipt validation, and refund handling are specified in the companion **Billing & Credits** documents and are out of scope for this MVP feature list. Phase 1 includes only the seams (account linkage and entitlement hooks) needed to adopt that system without rework.

## 8. Dependencies & Risks

- AI provider latency and cost directly affect conversation quality and the future credit model.
- Theme consistency requires a shared design-token layer across both platforms.
- Admin conversation inspection must ship with strict audit logging to preserve user trust.

## 9. Launch Criteria

All P0 items complete; sign-up-to-first-message flow verified on both platforms in both themes; admin moderation verified end to end; billing seams reviewed against the Billing & Credits docs.
